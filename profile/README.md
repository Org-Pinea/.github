## About Pinea

Pinea is a French brand specialising in custom mouse pads and large-format desk
mats. Our team operates from Lille, while our registered office is located in
Paris. We support individuals, creators, teams and businesses from the first
idea through to the finished product.

Our ambition is to bring together design, print quality and an effortless
customisation experience, turning an everyday accessory into a defining part
of any setup.

## What we create

- A visual customiser for uploading artwork and choosing a format and finish.
- Original collections ready for gaming setups and workspaces.
- Tailored solutions for businesses, teams, creators and events.
- A complete e-commerce experience, from product discovery to order tracking.

## What we stand for

- **Human support** — every project can be reviewed and guided before
  production.
- **Accessible customisation** — our experience makes it simple to turn an
  image into an order-ready product.
- **High-quality finishes** — visual impact and everyday performance matter in
  equal measure.
- **Continuous improvement** — our products and tools evolve around the real
  needs of our customers and partners.

## Contributing

The repositories in this organisation power our products and internal tools.

If you discover a security vulnerability, please do not disclose it in a
public issue. Contact us privately so that we can investigate it responsibly.
